Notes that apply to all of them:
- The site does not print a "type" label. The Type below is what the draw entries show (single players, named pairs, or teams).
- Each competition page is a knockout bracket (SportsPress tournament). Brackets were NOT scraped match by match; only the items below were read, once each, and the pairings quoted are "as read", not double-checked.
- The index page gives each competition a date (the page's publish date) and a strap-line; several strap-lines are stale copies from older seasons (e.g. the Shootout page says "Latest Fixtures & Results from the Bill Toms Competition 2024-2025"). They are not reliable descriptions.
- Dates such as 06/11/2026 19:30 or 30/12/2026 19:30 on first-round matches are the "play by" deadline entered as the match date, not real appointment times.
